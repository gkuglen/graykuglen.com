## The Problem with Most Real Estate Calculators

Most real estate calculators are complex, requiring detailed inputs that slow down early-stage decision-making. This creates friction when investors need to quickly assess whether an opportunity is even worth pursuing.

Time spent gathering data for a tool that ultimately says "no" is time wasted.

## A Faster Way to Screen

[Property Track](https://property-analyzer-getrealready.replit.app/) simplifies the process by focusing on three core indicators:

- **Profit potential** — is this property generating meaningful income relative to its price?
- **Return on investment** — how does the yield stack up against alternatives?
- **Market comparison** — how does this deal compare to the broader market?

Using just two inputs—purchase price and annual income—the tool provides a fast, directional view of whether a property is worth deeper analysis.

## Built for Early-Stage Screening

Property Track isn't meant to replace detailed financial modeling. It's the layer before it.

By reducing the initial evaluation to its most essential signals, investors can move faster through a pipeline of opportunities and reserve detailed analysis for properties that actually clear the bar.

Currently used as an early-stage screening layer to identify promising investments before applying more rigorous financial modeling.
