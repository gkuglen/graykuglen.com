## The Problem with Early-Stage Development Decisions

Real estate development is often seen as complex, capital-intensive, and difficult to navigate—creating barriers for smaller investors and limiting housing supply. Most decision-making tools require detailed inputs long before a project is ready for that level of rigor.

## A Simpler Way to Evaluate Feasibility

Property Developer focuses on a few critical levers that determine whether a project is worth pursuing:

- **Density potential** — including modular construction strategies that can reduce cost and timeline
- **Cash flow and asset value creation** — what does the stabilized asset look like?
- **Return on investment** — Cash-on-Cash return relative to capital deployed
- **Feasibility relative to construction costs** — does the math work before going deeper?

By estimating development costs and potential returns from a small set of inputs, the tool provides a directional view of project viability before engaging in deeper financial modeling.

## Built for Alignment, Not Just Analysis

Property Developer isn't just a calculator — it's a presentation layer. It's currently used as an early-stage evaluation and alignment tool to bring investors and property owners to the same page around development opportunities and financial outcomes before committing to detailed due diligence.

The goal is to compress the time between "is this worth looking at?" and a shared understanding of the numbers.
